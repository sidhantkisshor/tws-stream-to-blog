# TWS Wealth OS Brand System – Key Guidelines

## Overview

TWS Wealth OS uses a dual-palette system for emotional warmth and precise usability, positioning itself as premium wealth education tech. There are two core palettes:

- **Sophisticated Warmth**: Main brand identity for marketing and first impressions (human, aspirational).
- **Midnight Architect**: For product environments (professional, focus, comfort).
- **Wealth Teal**: Bridge color used in both to ensure visual continuity.

---

## Palette Essentials

**Sophisticated Warmth**  
- Deep Slate: Primary neutral, logo, text, backgrounds (#2C3539).
- Burnt Amber: Main accent for CTAs, headlines (#C87533).
- Brushed Gold: Premium accents for achievements and premium features, use sparingly (#B8956A).
- Warm White: Dominant light background (#FAF8F5).

**Midnight Architect**  
- Deep Navy-Black: Dark product interface base (#0B1221).
- Wealth Teal: Functional accent and cross-palette bridge (#0A8D7A).
- Soft Sand: Text and cards on dark backgrounds (#EDE6D8).
- Paper Cream: Light mode background for product/docs (#FFF1E0).

---

## Application Ratios

- **Brand/Marketing**:  
  70% Deep Slate/Warm White, 20% white space, 10% Burnt Amber/Brushed Gold.

- **Product/Dashboard**:  
  70% Navy-Black/Soft Sand, 20% white space, 10% Wealth Teal/Burnt Amber.

---

## Usage by Touchpoint

- **Website/Marketing**: Sophisticated Warmth. Use Deep Slate, Burnt Amber, Brushed Gold, Wealth Teal (links).
- **App/Dashboard**: Midnight Architect. Use Navy-Black, Soft Sand, Wealth Teal (CTA), Burnt Amber for achievements.
- **Emails**:  
  - Marketing: Warm White, Deep Slate, Burnt Amber, Wealth Teal (links).  
  - Product: Paper Cream, Navy-Black header, Wealth Teal/Graphite Ink text.
- **Social/Print**: Brand palette for all outward communication. Gold for premium touches only.

---

## Key Rules & Mistakes to Avoid

- Don’t use Midnight Architect palette in marketing.
- Don’t overuse Burnt Amber or Brushed Gold; use Gold <10%.
- Always use Wealth Teal as a bridge for continuity.
- Check all color contrasts for accessibility (WCAG AA).

---

## Typography

- **Brand (Sophisticated Warmth)**: Inter or Outfit, Burnt Amber for headlines, Deep Slate for body, Wealth Teal for links.
- **Product (Midnight Architect)**: Inter, Soft Sand for primary, Wealth Teal for data highlights.

---

## Quick Decisions

- Marketing/customer-facing? → Sophisticated Warmth
- Product/long-term use/data? → Midnight Architect
- Need transition/bridge? → Use Wealth Teal in both

---

## Checklist Before Launch

- Correct palette for environment and touchpoint
- Wealth Teal is present
- Brushed Gold is used sparingly
- All colors match hex codes
- Contrast meets accessibility
- Visuals feel warm (brand) or precise (product) as intended
