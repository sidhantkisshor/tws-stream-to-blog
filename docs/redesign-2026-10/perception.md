# Perception card: TWS blog redesign (index and post)

Written before any design work, 2026-10-30.

## Before

- Surface: web pages, the blog index and a post.
- Where they come from: YouTube description, Instagram bio or DM link, Google search.
- Arrival state: phone, at night, tired. Smart, has probably lost money, half-distrusts the finfluencers he follows. Expects another guru site.
- One message: "These people show the real trade, including the ones that did not work."
- Noticing order (index): 1st the giant line, 2nd the real chart with a calm serif title, 3rd a list worth reading.
- Noticing order (post with a chart): 1st the calm serif title over the real session chart, 2nd the giant hard line, 3rd the first paragraph.
- Allowed to miss: tags, contents list, share buttons, footer links.
- Action: keep reading. Then Telegram, at the end, not before.
- Feel 5 seconds later: "This is not what I expected. This feels expensive, and they are not selling me."
- Feel after they leave, later today: smarter, let in on how it works. Remembers the one line.
- Twist: the "photograph" behind the headline is not a photograph. It is the actual candles of that session with the actual entry line on it. You look twice, then you see the number from the headline.
- Voice temperature: calm and dry, stakes high, temperature low. One blunt line is the only time the page raises its voice.
- Visual moves: light serif titles, calm sans body, real margin, straight edges. One condensed black all-caps line per page. One hot accent, used only for the number in the shout and the level on the chart. Dark always means the market, light always means the reading.
- Opening experience: first paint is text, no fade on the big line, because it is the phone LCP. Link preview card shows the hard line, not a cream card or a P&L thumbnail.
- Trust cue near the ask: the data line under the chart ("Binance spot, 15 minute candles, session of 29 Oct 2026"). A receipt, never a hedge.

## Gap test

Two rounds, six fresh cold-readers on the phone first fold (glance only, night only). Round one used the 81,700 post, round two the gold VWAP post. These are model readers: a proxy for hierarchy and legibility, not proof that a person stops scrolling.
